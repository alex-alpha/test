# Integration Points

## lk.s-alpha.ru Features to Integrate

### Calculator
- **Location**: https://lk.s-alpha.ru/calculator
- **Purpose**: Compact B2B portal calculator
- **Features needed**:
  - Service selection
  - Rental vs Purchase options
  - Price estimation
  - Contact form submission

### Personal Cabinet
- **Login flow** from lk.s-alpha.ru
- **Dashboard** with:
  - Order management
  - Price lists
  - Delivery points
  - Document access

## Cross-Linking Strategy

### Content → Services
- Blog articles link to relevant services
- Case studies link to implementation services
- Glossary terms link to service pages

### Services → Content
- Service pages link to related articles
- Technical documentation in blog
- How-to guides for each service

### Calculator → Services
- Calculator results link to service details
- Pricing page links to calculator
